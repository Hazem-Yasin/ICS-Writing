# Scene Notes — The Review Room

*Level 2, Room 3. The Step 1 conversation between Sera Vasquez and Kai. A warm room, a good tea, and a fact the player can only see from the outside.*

---

## Where and when

Level 2, HR & Wellness, Room 3: the round table, the plant, the window onto the atrium, the clock that is two minutes fast. (`canon/facility/Level 2.md`.)

The scene arrives when the hidden Plan meter reaches 30 (`design/PLAN_SYSTEM.md` §6) and Sera's message A-31 (Day 24) is read. The meeting is the Thursday that follows, Day 30, in Phase 5. It is a Conversation, short, scripted, with player choices that change nothing the player can measure.

*→ See: A-31 — Sera to Kai, "A quick conversation, if you have twenty minutes".*
*→ See: Document 2.53 (Support Steps Manual) §3 — the script Sera is using.*

---

## The room

Close third, present tense.

Kai arrives at 14:00 by the room's clock. By the corridor's clock it is 13:58. Sera is already there. The tea is already poured, in two cups that match, and one of them is the good tea. The plant has been watered today. The window shows the atrium and the people crossing it, and from here none of them look up.

Sera stands when Kai comes in. She does not stand for everyone.

She says it is not a warning. She says it before Kai has sat down. She says she has a few of these each week and enjoys them. All of this is true, and Kai can hear that it is true, which is what makes it difficult to listen to.

---

## What happens

Sera asks three questions. They are the questions in the template, and she does not look at the template.

1. *How are you finding things?*
2. *Is there anything that would make the work feel more like yours?*
3. *Is there anything you'd like me to make easier?*

Whatever Kai answers, Sera writes one line on a pad. The pad is paper. She does not turn it round, and she does not hide it either. The line is short.

If Kai asks what the conversation is for, Sera tells the truth as far as she has it: *"I was asked to have it. I don't know everything behind that. I'd tell you if I did."* Both sentences are accurate. The second is the kindest thing anyone in the room can say, and it is the only place in the scene where the player might hear the building behind her.

At the end she offers a second conversation, "in a few weeks, if it helps." She says *if it helps*. The date is already in the system.

---

## What the player can see, and what they cannot

The player can see:

- The clock is two minutes fast.
- The pad is paper, and the pen is the Wellness Hub's own.
- Sera's tea goes cold. She does not drink it.
- Her hand is steady until the second question, and then it is slightly less so.

The player cannot see:

- That the outcome field for this conversation was never empty.
- That Sera's template carries no field for *what Kai said*, only *whether Kai attended*.
- That the Wellness Hub will show Kai a goal-attainment bar afterwards, and that the bar stops at 80%.

The player may learn one of these later, from the My Growth tab (`design/UI_MENUS.md`), or from B-35, or from A-37. None of them is explained here.

---

## What the scene is not

It is not an interrogation. Nobody raises a voice. Nobody says *Plan*, *Step*, *PIP* or *ceiling*. Sera says *chat* once, and *opportunity* once, and neither is a lie.

The horror is that it is a good conversation. Kai leaves a little lighter. The tea was good. The player has just been told, by someone who likes them, that something has begun, and the only evidence is that it felt kind.

---

## Last beat

Kai stands. Sera stands. At the door Sera says, *"Thank you for coming. I mean it."* She does mean it.

In the corridor Kai looks at the clock. It is 14:22. The room's clock said 14:24.

For about a minute, the player has gained two minutes.
