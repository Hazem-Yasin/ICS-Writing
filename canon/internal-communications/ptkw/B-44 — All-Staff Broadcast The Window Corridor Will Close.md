> **A Difficult Message — From CLEO**
>
> *"CLEO Says: 'A Shared Space Is Only As Strong As The Care We Give It.'"*
>
> ---
>
> Hello, ICS Family.
>
> We have a difficult message to share, and we would like to share it honestly.
>
> When three new seats were installed at the Earth View Window, they were a gift from the Branch to every one of you. They were a sign of how much we value your time away from your work.
>
> Over the last few weeks, the corridor has had to close four times. Twice because food was left on the floor and on the glass. Twice because a seat's tilt function was used in a way it was not designed for. Every one of those closures took a team out of their night. Every one of them took the window away from the colleagues who were looking forward to it.
>
> We are not here to point fingers. We are here because a space that is not cared for cannot be kept.
>
> **What happens now:**
>
> - The three new seats have been withdrawn, with immediate effect.
> - The corridor stays open, with the original bench only.
> - **The Earth View Window corridor will close to staff on the first day of Cycle 301 Q1.** This decision has been made with care and cannot be reversed.
> - If you have a concern about this decision, please share it with your Wellness Coordinator. They are always happy to listen.
>
> Until then, the bench is yours. Please use it kindly. Please keep food and sleep to the cafeteria and your quarters, where they belong.
>
> You are ICS. A thing we look after is a thing we get to keep. Almost always.
>
> *CLEO Says: "Understanding Your Role Is The First Step To Excelling In It."*
>
> With warmth,
> **ICS Earth Branch — People & Culture Division**
