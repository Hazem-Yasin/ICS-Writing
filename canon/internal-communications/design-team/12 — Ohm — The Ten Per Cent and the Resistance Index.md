Akar,

I ran the Contribution Distribution against the resistance-index projections (memo 04). I wanted to see whether the Calibration Round changes the numbers. It does, and not in the direction we expected.

**The result.** On floors where the bottom band is filled, the resistance index rises in the next quarter by about 0.1 points. On floors where it is not filled, there is no rise. The Round, which was meant to contain the index by removing the people who raise it, raises it.

**Why.** The bottom band is not drawn from the people with the highest resistance. It is drawn from the people with the strongest bonds, which correlate with warmth, with loyalty, with having a floor to lose. When a floor loses one of these, the floor's remaining members start asking why. They ask quietly. They ask in small groups of three, which is the size that the Policy asks managers to report (Document 2.30, §3).

**What I believe.** I think we have built an instrument that does two things at once. It removes a person, and it makes three others a little more awake. The first is what the Board wants. The second is what the Board wants more.

I checked the projections against the Board's note (design-team 07). The weighting for resistance nodes at Earth is 3.2 times standard. A floor that loses one warm colleague and gains three awake ones is, on the Board's arithmetic, a gain.

I would like to say that I raised the concern and was overruled. I have not raised it. I am raising it now, here, to you.

If there is a version of this in which I did something other than build the instrument, I would like to hear it.

— Ohm
