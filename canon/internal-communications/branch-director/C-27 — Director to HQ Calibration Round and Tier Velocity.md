Cluster Representative Brant,

I am writing to ask for a hold on one floor, for one round, and I would like to be exact about why.

Floor B has been the Branch's most stable floor for three quarters. Its manager returned the Calibration form with no colleague placed in Support Needed. The Facilitator has asked for a second look, and has indicated that the floor may be pooled with Floor A at the next round.

I do not see how the Branch is better served by placing a colleague from a floor that is working, in order to meet a figure that was set for floors that are not. I would like to request that Floor B be excused from the ten per cent for this round, and that the Tier Velocity Standard be read as a guide rather than a count.

I note that I have said "guide" twice. I am aware that this is the Branch's word for it, and not HQ's.

I would be grateful for a view. I will of course carry out whatever is directed.

— Maren Voss
Branch Director, ICS Earth Branch

**Reply — Day 7, 15:10**

Director Voss,

Thank you for your message and for the care in it. HQ shares your admiration for Floor B.

The request is declined. The distribution is applied in the same proportion to every floor, every quarter, so that no floor is seen to be treated differently. A floor that is excused becomes a floor that is noticed.

On the Tier Velocity Standard: the review points are a count. The Branch is not asked to explain them.

You write that you will carry out whatever is directed. HQ has noted this and finds it reassuring.

With warm regards,
K. Brant
