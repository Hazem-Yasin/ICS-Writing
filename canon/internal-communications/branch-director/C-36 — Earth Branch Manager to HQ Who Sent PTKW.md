I emptied the floor so that nobody would see him. I sealed the north face myself. I closed every recorder but two. Thirty-three of ours stood on that floor tonight. They are not the Directorate. They live here. At seven they will be in the Level 5 canteen, with their trays.

I will say what I saw once, to you. He was down for nine seconds and the Directorate held. He got up. After that they did not hold, and I have no count of the next minutes that I trust.

I would also like to know whether anyone told you that he asked for nothing. He came to the south door with his hands open. It was the Directorate that did not know what it was looking at.

— Earth Branch Manager
