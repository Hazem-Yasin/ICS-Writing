**From:** Earth Branch Operations (Earth Branch Manager) <manager@e349.ics>
**To:** ICS Interplanetary Research Division (EOD Research Team) <eod-research@hq.ics>
**Subject:** The sheet in his quarters, and the second decision
**Date:** Cycle 300 Q4 — Day 34, 07:40
**Status:** SEALED — TWO READERS: EOD Research Team, Earth Branch Manager. As C-24, C-25 and C-30. EOD is not a reader.

---

Team,

At 07:05 this morning I entered his quarters on Level 13 for the first time. I went to walk them before the Day 35 review, as I said in C-25 that I would walk the floor. On the desk, face up, in the middle, there was one leaf of ruled paper written in pencil on both sides. Nothing lay on it. The pencil lay beside it. I have transcribed it in full as Document 2.63. The original stays where I found it.

Please read 2.63 before you read the rest of this message.

---

**1. What it is**

It is a record of what he did on the planet, made by hand, in a form that no instrument of the Division reads. It is not addressed. It says that it will be read. I think he knew who would read it, and in which room.

The ARM-27 log is silent for Days 22 to 28. I had taken that for the ten days' silence carried over, as GOV-PDR-004 allows. I now read it as a second absence, shorter than the first, spent on the planet. Your order in C-24 began the log on Day 29. The log since then places him in the bay or the corridor and has him speaking to no one. I have no entry for the quarters, because nothing took him there. The sheet was left before the log started. He has not left his instructions or the schedule since I was told to watch. Trigger (d) has not occurred.

---

**2. The triggers**

C-25 says that if any one of the five occurs I will give you the second decision the same day and not argue it. I have gone through the five against the sheet.

- **(a)** *The sentence, or a paraphrase.* "They cannot be moved by the thing I am for." That is "we can't control them," written down and left where it would be read. It is not said to anyone. I count it. The Team will say that a sheet is a channel.
- **(b)** *A state of his own, in any word.* "Amazed." He wrote that none of his words was accurate except that one, and that he had no entry for it.
- **(c)** *The Captain's face, or the smile.* He answers the question 2.49 is named for. He did it without being asked. Standing instruction 3 holds: he has not been asked again, and he has told us anyway.
- **(d)** Not met.
- **(e)** *The anticipation reading.* I do not know. The reading has been at baseline throughout the log. I am not told what the instrument would show for a man who has settled something.

(a), (b) and (c) are met. One would have ended the first option. Three do not need a second reading.

---

**3. My decision**

The second option. I do not argue it, and I will not ask the Team to hold the order for Day 35. The review is moot, since it was to be a review of the log, and the log has been answered by the one who kept it.

I said I would comply to the letter. The six items of C-25 stand as I set them out: Level 7, the whole floor; Day 38, 02:00; the recertification pretext; no asset losses; thirty-one days to repair, nineteen of them for the bay; nobody to see the engagement. The final figures reach you today.

I ask the Team to issue the confirmation at 02:00 on Day 35, so that Level 7 closes with three days in hand and not four.

---

**4. What I ask**

Two things, both from C-25 section 4.

1. That the message goes to him on the PTKW channel as I drafted it, in my name. The sheet makes me surer of this than I was. He has written that he does not know whether fear has a floor. He should hear the Branch ask him for it.
2. That the floor is not rearranged, and that the cover is not broken. I add one thing to the second: that nothing in the engagement is done to him in the first minute that he could mistake for the specification.

I have nothing else to ask. I find that I would have liked to.

— The Earth Branch Manager

---

**Reply — Day 34, 11:50**

Manager,

The Team has read the sheet and your message. The decision is received at 11:15 and is recorded as yours.

1. The confirmation will issue on Day 35, 02:00. Level 7 closes at that hour. The hour of the engagement is Day 38, 02:00. The Directorate has been told.
2. The PTKW message is granted, in your name, as drafted. The floor will not be rearranged. The cover will not be broken, and the Directorate will answer him as the Consortium and as no one else.
3. The last thing he hears is for the Directorate to judge. The Team will neither ask for it nor forbid it.
4. The Team notes that the sheet is the first document it has received from him that is not the answer to a question. It does not know what to call it. It files it with 2.49 as 2.63.
5. The Team will not read the sheet a second time. It asks that you do not either.

— EOD Research Team
