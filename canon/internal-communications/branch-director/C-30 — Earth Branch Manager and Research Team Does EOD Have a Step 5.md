Dear Team,

I have been reading the Support Steps Manual (Document 2.53) because the Branch has had a month of it in front of my desk. I have a question and I would like you to take it as seriously as I mean it.

Do you do this to him?

The Directorate's termination specification was lodged at his creation as a standing instruction (C-24). It is a Step 5 with no Steps 1 to 4. It was written to fire, not to be experienced. I am not asking you to do it. I am asking whether you have thought of it, and what you decided.

I would also like to ask, though I know it is not mine to ask: if the Manual was written by someone who knew what it was doing, why does it read so much like love?
