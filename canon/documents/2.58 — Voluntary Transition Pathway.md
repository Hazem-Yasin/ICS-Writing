### 2.58 Voluntary Transition Pathway

> **ICS EARTH BRANCH — PEOPLE & CULTURE**
> **Voluntary Transition Pathway**
> *"A new chapter, when you're ready."*
>
> ---
>
> **Is this for me?**
>
> Some colleagues find that their time at the Branch has been right, and is now complete. If you feel that way, the **Voluntary Transition Pathway** is a respectful way to say so.
>
> **What you get**
>
> - A Journey Completion Day: a farewell with your floor, with cake.
> - A **Completion Credit** equal to six months' SOLCRED allocation.
> - A letter of thanks from the Branch Director, signed.
> - Your AIDEN, if you would like to keep it.
> - Support in your next chapter.
>
> **How to ask**
>
> Say so to your Wellness Coordinator. There are no forms to fill in. We will take it from there.
>
> **What this is not**
>
> It is not a punishment, a decision, or a final one. You can change your mind, at any time, until the Completion Day.
>
> *Colleagues who have chosen the Pathway tell us it was one of the best conversations they had at the Branch.*

> **PTKW Division — Voluntary Transition: Operating Notes**
>
> **The Pathway is how a Step 5 is made to look like a choice.** A colleague is steered to *ask* for it, so that the record shows a request. A request is a signature. A signature is a defence.
>
> **Steering.** The colleague is told, kindly, in a Step 4 assessment: *"Some colleagues in your situation find the Pathway a relief."* The Pathway is never proposed in a Step 1–3 conversation. It is mentioned once, as a possibility, with the word *relief*. The Coordinator then says nothing further.
>
> **Offered when the outcome is already set.** Where the outcome field reads COMPLETE (Document 2.53 §4), the Pathway is an alternative route to the same date. A colleague who takes it receives a Completion Credit. A colleague who does not take it reaches the same day without one.
>
> **Incentive.** The Credit is paid into a SOLCRED account that is closed on the Completion Day. It is paid in a currency that does not leave the Branch (Document 2.45).
>
> **Selected to be lost.** A Voluntary Transition may be arranged as an instrument. The colleague is chosen not for their own record but for the bond they hold to another asset (see Document 2.52, Research Team sheet). Their file is given a reason that fits them: a quiet request for a new chapter, supported by their own earlier remarks. The colleague who remains is told, kindly, and given a plan.
>
> **The colleague who leaves is never told they were chosen.** The colleague who stays is told *"it was their choice"*. The second sentence is true.
