> **PTKW DIVISION — Belonging Pulse: Analysis Summary (Q4)**
>
> | Item | Purpose in analysis |
> |---|---|
> | 1 | Comprehension of the facility's rules |
> | 2 | Strength of the manager's grip on the team |
> | 3 | **Bond count.** Items 3 and 4 together form the Mosaic Dependency indicator (Document 2.29) |
> | 4 | Intent to stay: a proxy for exit-deterrence success |
> | 5 | **Suppression index.** A high score here is a risk, not a comfort: it marks respondents who have decided what not to say |
> | 6 | **Cynicism index.** A low score marks respondents who have stopped expecting change |
> | 7 | Compliance with the "acting-on-concern" narrative |
> | 8 | Whether the respondent still tells Earth a story that suits us |
>
> **Free-text box.** Entries are routed to the EIMU linguistic layer. Words from a list of 212 trigger terms produce a Protocol 7 flag at the respondent's next check-in.
>
> **Responses are not anonymous.** They are *confidential* in the sense that managers do not see them. Reporting at group level is a view of the data, not a limit on it.
>
> **Group size for reporting:** five. Groups of five or fewer are merged upward so that no individual is shown. The unmerged data is retained at asset level.
>
> **Observation, Q3 → Q4:** respondents who completed the survey in full were 11% less likely to open a Protocol 7 flag in the following 30 days. Respondents who skipped Item 5 were 40% more likely. *The survey reduces the behaviour it measures. This is a result.*
>
> **The 'You said. We did.' line** is produced from the three most frequent free-text words that have no operational cost. In Q3 the word was "music". It cost nothing.

> **Research Team note**
>
> The most useful finding of the programme is a negative one. Where the Branch has acted on a survey item, the item's score has *fallen* the following quarter. A concern that has been answered stops being a concern; the respondent stops raising it, and the signal is lost. A concern that has been heard and not acted on persists, and persistence is where the depth is.
>
> Hence: do not act. Be seen to listen.
>
> **Correlation, Items 3 and 4 against Support Step 5, last four cycles.** A respondent who scores 5 on Item 3 (a close friend) and, in the following quarter, loses that friend to a Step 5 shows a 38% rise in output over the next 40 days. The rise is largest where the lost friend was the respondent's only 5.
>
> The survey identifies who has a bond worth cutting. The cut is arranged elsewhere.
