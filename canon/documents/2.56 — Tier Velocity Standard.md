### 2.56 Tier Velocity Standard

*[A one-page notice posted on the People & Culture board on Level 2 and sent to every manager. The supplementary page is PTKW-only and was found stapled behind the board copy. Revision 3.]*

---

> **ICS EARTH BRANCH — PEOPLE & CULTURE**
> **Tier Velocity Standard**
> *"Growth is the Branch's way of saying thank you."*
>
> ---
>
> **What it is**
>
> A Tier is a recognition of growth. The Tier Velocity Standard describes how quickly the Branch hopes to recognise it. It is a shared expectation, not a deadline.
>
> | Tier | Typical review point |
> |---|---|
> | Tier 3 (new) | Quarterly review begins after the Settling-In Period (Document 2.57) |
> | Tier 2 | Within 8 quarters of reaching Tier 3 |
> | Tier 1 | Within 12 quarters of reaching Tier 2 |
>
> **If your review point passes**
>
> Your Wellness Coordinator will get in touch for a conversation about *what you would like next*. This is a growth opportunity, not a concern. Colleagues who prefer a different pace are welcome to ask for a **Voluntary Transition** (Document 2.58) at any time.
>
> **Why we do this**
>
> A Branch where no one moves is a Branch where no one is growing. We think everyone is capable of more.

> **PTKW Division — Supplement to Tier Velocity Standard**
>
> **Velocity is capped, not just encouraged.** Advancement beyond Tier 1 is not available at the Branch, and advancement to Tier 1 is rationed. The number of Tier 1 places in any quarter is fixed. A colleague who reaches a review point cannot advance if no place is open; and a place does not open unless a colleague leaves one.
>
> **A colleague who is not advancing is a signal in itself.** Colleagues at the review point for three consecutive quarters without advancement are flagged **Velocity Stall (VS)**. A VS flag opens Support Step 1 (Document 2.53) without a Calibration placement.
>
> **The stall is manufactured.** The review point is published; the number of places is not. The rationing of Tier 1 ensures that, at any time, about one colleague in seven at Tier 2 is in a VS flag through no act of their own.
>
> **Tier 4.** A colleague moved to Tier 4 is no longer subject to the Standard. Nothing is expected of them. Access, meal credits and leisure time are adjusted (see Document 2.08) *without announcement*. A colleague on Tier 4 is not told they are on it for 14 days, and is told then, in a conversation, that they have "been given time".
>
> **Quiet reclassification.** A colleague whose tier is lowered has their badge exchanged at the next scheduled exchange, not before. Between the decision and the exchange, the old badge works. Doors do not close all at once; they stop opening one at a time, starting with the ones that matter least.
