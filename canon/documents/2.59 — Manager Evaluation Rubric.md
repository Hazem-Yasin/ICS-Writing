### 2.59 Manager Evaluation Rubric — Care Outcomes

*[Access: PTKW — Restricted. A single page, found in the Level 2 manager corridor under a pot plant. The version managers are given is called "Your Manager Growth Conversation" and includes only the first column of the table.]*

---

> **ICS EARTH BRANCH — PEOPLE & CULTURE / PTKW DIVISION**
> **Manager Evaluation Rubric**
> *Document Ref: EB-HRW-07 | Revision 2*
> *Classification: PTKW ACCESS ONLY*
>
> ---
>
> **Scoring (quarterly, out of 100)**
>
> | Category | What managers are told | What it measures | Weight |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **Warmth** | Your team's mood and cohesion | Floor stability index (low variance in EIMU readings) | 15 |
> | **Advancement** | Colleagues you have helped to grow | Colleagues moved to Support Step 5 that quarter, measured against target | 30 |
> | **Candour** | How honest your ratings are | Share of the team placed in *Support Needed* (Document 2.54) | 25 |
> | **Alignment** | Whether you follow the Branch's lead | Time between a flag being raised on your floor and your Coordinator's follow-up | 15 |
> | **Presence** | Visibility on your floor | Time with the office glass clear | 15 |
>
> ---
>
> **Notes**
>
> **Advancement** is the Care Outcomes line. It counts colleagues who completed a Support Step 5 *with the manager's agreement on record*. Managers who contest a Step are scored zero for the quarter on this line, and a Candour deduction applies.
>
> **A manager who advocates for a colleague on Step 3 or later** is recorded under **Alignment** as *"attachment"*. Two entries in a year open a Support Step 1 for the manager.
>
> **A manager whose floor shows no movement** (no Step 5, no new Step 3 plans) is flagged **Stability Concern** and receives a facilitated session. The session is about *energy*.
>
> **A manager who meets all targets** is rewarded with a Tier 1 review point and a Care Commendation.
>
> ---
>
> **Why the columns differ**
>
> The Branch has found that a manager who knows they are scored on the number of colleagues who leave behaves as a manager who is selling. A manager who thinks they are scored on mood and growth behaves as a carer, and is a better carer: they are warm, they cry at the farewells, and they mean it. The rubric is therefore not shown.
>
> Cases of a manager who learns the true column are handled by Support Step 1. Where the manager was valued, they are given a Care Commendation and the knowledge that they cannot speak of it.

> **Board note**
>
> The Board has noted a result. Managers who do not know what they are scored on perform better against it than those who do. They give more of themselves to the work and less to the arithmetic. They also suffer afterwards in ways which the Branch has found useful.
>
> The manager who holds a Step 5 farewell for a colleague they have supported, believing it a graduation, and who then sees the desk empty the next morning, is the Branch's most reliable source of the sentence *"I did what I could"*.
