### 2.57 Settling-In Period — Guidance for Line Managers

*[A laminated card in the front pocket of the manager induction folder, with a longer PTKW note on the back. Revision 5.]*

---

> **ICS EARTH BRANCH — PEOPLE & CULTURE**
> **The Settling-In Period: A Guide for Line Managers**
> *"The first ninety days are the start of a long friendship."*
>
> ---
>
> **What it is**
>
> Every new colleague has a Settling-In Period of 90 days from the day their badge changes. During it, you are their first point of contact. Your job is to help them feel they belong.
>
> **What to do**
>
> - **Day 1:** welcome, walk the floor, introduce AIDEN.
> - **Weeks 1–4:** a daily two-minute check-in. Ask how they slept.
> - **Week 6:** a Settling-In Conversation. Bring tea.
> - **Day 90:** the Settling-In Review. A colleague who has settled is confirmed in post.
>
> **If a colleague is not settling**
>
> Some colleagues take longer. The period may be extended in 30-day units *for the colleague's benefit*. An extension is not a concern. Please say so, and say it more than once.
>
> **What the colleague can expect**
>
> The Branch will not ask a colleague in their Settling-In Period to make any decision that cannot be undone.

> **PTKW Division — Back of card**
>
> **During the period, the Branch may end employment without a Support Step.** A colleague in the Settling-In Period is not yet in the process described in Document 2.53; they are in an earlier one. Their file is reviewed daily by the Behavioral Data Analysis unit, and a Settling-In flag may be closed as **"Not settling"** at any time.
>
> **What "Not settling" means.** The colleague is moved directly to Support Step 4 (ICS Wellness Assessment). Steps 1 to 3 are skipped. The manager is told the colleague "has been given support" and is not told the step. The manager is asked to keep the colleague's desk clear for the Journey Completion conversation.
>
> **Why the period exists.** The first 90 days are the Branch's best window for reading a colleague's baseline. A colleague who is unsuited to the Branch, or whose profile does not agree with the intake assessment, is identified early. The rest are *extended* to hold their baseline open as long as possible. Extension is not kindness. It is a longer observation.
>
> **Three questions that produce a flag in the first 90 days:**
> 1. Where do the lights go at night?
> 2. Who decides the music?
> 3. What happened to the person who sat here before?
>
> **The Settling-In flag does not require a reason.** A colleague's file may state *"personal fit"*.
>
> **The manager is asked, on Day 90, to write a sentence about the colleague.** The sentence is kept. If the colleague is not confirmed, it is read to them.
