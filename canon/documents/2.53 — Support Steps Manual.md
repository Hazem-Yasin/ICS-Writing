> **1. What the steps are for**
>
> The Support Steps give a colleague a clear, kind and predictable route through a difficult period. A colleague who knows what happens next is calmer than one who does not. Every step is designed to be something the colleague can say yes to.
>
> No step is a penalty. The word *penalty* is not used in any conversation, form or message in this process. If a colleague uses it, do not correct them. Say: *"I'm hearing that this feels heavy. Let's talk about that."*
>
> ---
>
> **2. The five steps**
>
> | Step | Name | Length | What the colleague is told |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **1** | Conversation | 20 min | *"This isn't a warning. It's a check-in."* |
> | **2** | Check-In | 20 min, 10–14 days after Step 1 | *"Following up, because we care how it's going."* |
> | **3** | Care Action Plan | 30 days (extendable) | *"A plan we agree together."* Form ICS-PG1 (Doc 2.52) |
> | **4** | ICS Wellness Assessment | 60 min | *"A full review of your situation, needs and options."* Form ICS-WA4 (Doc 2.61) |
> | **5** | Journey Completion | 1 day | *"A celebration of everything you've contributed."* |
>
> Steps 1 and 2 are not entered on the colleague's Branch record (Doc 2.30, Part Three). They are entered on the PTKW contact log, which the colleague does not see. The colleague is told, truthfully, that nothing is on the record.
>
> ---
>
> **3. Conversation scripts**
>
> Say: *"We've noticed."* *"How are you finding things?"* *"What would help?"* *"There's no wrong answer."* *"You're not in trouble."*
>
> Do not say: *"performance"*, *"improvement"*, *"concern"* (as a noun), *"formal"*, *"record"*, *"HR"* (say *People & Culture*), *"final"*, *"outcome"*.
>
> If the colleague asks whether this is going to be a problem for them: *"What I'd like is for us to make it not one."*
>
> If the colleague asks what happens if the plan doesn't work: *"Plans are adjusted, not failed. We'd adjust it together."*
>

> **4. The outcome field**
>
> Every file carries an outcome field. It is entered by Interplanetary HQ, not by the Branch, and it is read-only at every Branch level below the Director. It holds one of three codes: **CONTINUE**, **EXTEND**, or **COMPLETE**. It is set before Step 1.
>
> The coordinator and the analyst do not see the field and are not to infer it. Both are told that the Branch's view of the colleague "informs" the outcome. The Branch's view does not inform the outcome. It is the evidence the file carries afterwards.
>
> A file's observed effort therefore has no bearing on its code. Do not tell a colleague this. A colleague who has been told effort does not matter stops giving effort, and the file loses value.
>
> ---
>
> **5. Timing**
>
> The Step 5 date is derived from the colleague's readiness curve (Research & Learning, Behavioral Data Analysis), not from the plan. The plan's length is set backward from the date. Plans are extended in 14-day units until the date is reached.
>
> Do not offer the colleague an end date for Step 5. Do not offer an end date for Step 4. The colleague plans around the next conversation, not around the last one.
>
> ---
>
> **6. Keeping the file complete**
>
> A file should always support its outcome. Where an observation is missing, the analyst may enter it after the event, dated to the event, and cite the Branch's standard of review ("reasonably expected to have been noted"). Observations entered after the fact carry the suffix **[A]** in the PTKW log. The suffix is not visible to the coordinator.
>
> Where a colleague's account of a meeting differs from the log, the log is not corrected. See Document 2.60 (Perception Alignment Protocol).
>
> ---
>
> **7. Completion**
>
> Fewer than one plan in six is completed. Do not treat this as a failing of the process. A completed plan closes the colleague's file at Step 3. A closed file yields no further observations. Coordinators are scored on the *continuation* of the support relationship, not on closure (Document 2.59).
>
> A colleague who completes a plan is offered a second plan "to keep the momentum".
>
> ---
>
> **8. Colleagues**
>
> A colleague who asks about another colleague on a Step, or who is seen with one repeatedly, receives a Step 1 Conversation of their own within 14 days. The conversation is about *their own wellbeing*.
>
> A colleague on Step 3 or later is not told that anyone else is on a Step. The Care Action Plan, the Wellness Hub tea credits and the weekly conversation are described as *personal*.

> **Appendix for the Research Team (not distributed)**
>
> The five steps are a delivery mechanism for a decision taken elsewhere. The Branch is a good place to run them: it is warm, the staff are kind, and the people who deliver the steps believe in them. A process run by believers reads, to its subject, as care.
>
> The reverse is also true. A process that was cold would be reported on. A process that is warm is experienced as a failure of the subject. The colleague on Step 3 who is told "this is not a warning" and who then works harder to avoid a warning has accepted the frame. That acceptance is the output.
