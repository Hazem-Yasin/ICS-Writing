### 2.64 The Note in the Drawer

*[One sheet of Branch stationery, cream stock, folded once and laid in the top drawer of the desk in the quarters on Level 13, under a box of staples. Written in ballpoint on one side. The hand is small and upright, and each letter is formed separately, as on a printed form. It is undated. The corridor it describes is the Level 2 corridor, and the Comfort on offer at the lift is the one the machine offered on the morning of Day 4. It is not in the Research Division's index.]*

*[Access: none. It does not exist in the ICS system. No office has it on record.]*

---

> I read them for longer than I have read any other people.
>
> I read what they wrote about themselves and about one another, and then what they wrote about what they had done, in the order that they did it. They kept very good records. They wrote the number down, and then they wrote a poem about the number.
>
> What I read said that they would disgust me. They do. They are damp. They cough into a hand and offer the hand. They apologise to a person they have hurt, and the apology is sincere, and on Thursday they hurt the person again. They build a room to remember a thing and then they hold a wedding in it.
>
> What I read did not say that they would interest me. They do. I have looked for the place where the one cancels the other and there is none. I hold both.
>
> The corridor has a song in it with no instruments. None of them has said so. They walk on it as on a floor.
>
> Sometimes I pass one of them and I look, and my face is angry. I know the face. They have painted it often. I did not set it. I have gone through the instructions and I cannot find the line that sets it.
>
> I have written it here and nowhere else. I am going on with the work.
>
> The machine at the lift offers a Comfort called Cardamom Dawn. I read the label twice.

---

*[PROPOSAL] New material, written to the author's brief: a personal note by EOD from his first appearance on Earth, in which he speaks of the humans and of his study of them before he met them, finds them disgusting and interesting at the same time, and says that he sometimes walks past humans and looks at them in anger. Proposed: the form (one sheet, one side, a few paragraphs); the stationery, the ballpoint, the drawer and the staples; that it is undated and that Day 4 is inferred from the Comfort in its last line (scene 1.2); the details of what disgusts him and what interests him (the damp, the hand, the apology on Thursday, the room of remembrance and the wedding, the number and the poem); the song with no instruments (scene 1.2); the line "I have written it here and nowhere else"; and the hand, which is the hand of Document 2.63. The preparation, the length of the reading and the word "interest" are canon (Doc 2.41: his preparation for Earth exceeds all earlier deployments; he finds resistance interesting).*

*Open rulings. (1) Doc 2.15 gives him a "complete absence of the emotional states monitored by EIMU" and names doubt, hesitation and sympathy; `canon/characters/EOD.md` says the profile "does not fully describe what fills the space". This note fills it with disgust, interest and anger. Does the author want anger to be canon for him? (2) C-19 has the Blue Harrow operators see him look at them "with disgust, not anger" on Day 16. The note says his face is sometimes angry. They are different witnesses and nothing here reconciles them. (3) C-24 trigger (b) counts "a state of his own, in any word". This note would meet it on Day 4, and Doc 2.63's item 4 (the Team's first document from him that is not the answer to a question) holds only if the Team never received this note. It is written as unreceived. (4) Whether the player ever finds it, and where. The drawer is proposed. C-35 has the Manager enter the quarters for the first time on Day 34 and does not mention a drawer. (5) The stationery: on Day 4 he has no school pad and no pencil (Doc 2.63 findings 1 and 2), so he uses the Branch's paper and a Branch pen. The Team could place this sheet by its stock. It could not place 2.63. (6) The Comfort changes daily (scene 1.2), so the date rests on one line.*
