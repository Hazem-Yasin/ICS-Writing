> **ASK A COLLEAGUE**
>
> Wondering about something? That's completely normal.
>
> Every colleague with a grey PTKW card is trained, equipped and glad to answer your questions. They know the facility. They know the contract. They know how things work, and they are not busy in the way that other people are busy.
>
> There is no such thing as a silly question. There is only a question that hasn't been asked yet.
>
> **How to ask**
> 1. Find the nearest grey card, or write to your Floor Liaison through CCN.
> 2. Ask in your own words.
> 3. Expect a kind and honest reply within one working day.
>
> *Your Floor Liaison is here to help.*
>
> *ICS: Because You're Worth the Investment.*

> **ICS EARTH BRANCH — PTKW DIVISION / FLOOR LIAISON OFFICE**
> **Floor Liaison Guide: Receiving Colleague Questions**
> *Document Ref: EB-PTKW-11 | Revision 4*
> *Classification: PTKW ACCESS ONLY*
>
> ---
>
> **1. Why the leaflet says what it says**
>
> Assets have been told since arrival (the *Lookup for the Second Moon* video, Document 2.04) to bring concerns to a PTKW colleague. The instruction is deliberate. It is the Branch's pressure release and its earliest detector (Document 2.21, Section 3). A colleague who has somewhere to take a question does not take it to a colleague. A colleague who asks you has told you what the EIMU has not yet read.
>
> The Floor Liaison is therefore not a security post. The Floor Liaison is the Branch's front desk for curiosity. In every respect that an asset can see, the role is indistinguishable from a good HR partner. You are warm, you are available, and you do not make anyone feel they have broken a rule by asking.
>
> ---
>
> **2. Who asks**
>
> You will be asked by two kinds of colleague, and the Branch is not equally interested in both.
>
> **The Unsettled.** A colleague who is unhappy, or frightened, or has lost someone. Their questions are loud, frequent and easy to read. The EIMU already has them. Most are routed to the Wellness Hub. They are not a concern for this office.
>
> **The Comfortable.** A colleague with a Stability Index above 0.8, a stable bond count, a good tier and a reputation for being easy to work beside. They do not usually ask. When they ask, they ask carefully, once, and in the tone of someone who is only curious. They have no complaint. They have nothing to report. The EIMU has no flag on them and is not expected to.
>
> A question from the Comfortable is entered as a **Comfortable Inquiry (CI)** and is never closed at this desk. The more settled the colleague, the more weight the question carries. A colleague with nothing to complain about who asks anyway has stopped being only comfortable.
>
> ---
>
> **3. The three rules of reply**
>
> 1. **Answer in full, up to the edge of the approved reply.** Never say *"I can't tell you."* Say what is true and complete enough to end the question. An answer that ends a question early is a question that has not yet been asked of someone else.
> 2. **Never ask why they asked.** A colleague asked *why* will conclude that the question was an event. Ask instead, *"What made you think of that today?"* Enter the answer.
> 3. **Be warm.** If a reply is cold, the colleague will repeat the question to a friend. The friend is a second record. See B-27 for a cold reply and its cost.
>
> ---
>
> **4. The ladder**
>
> | Rung | What it is | Who decides |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Answer** | An approved reply from the Floor Liaison's reply book. Enter as CONTACT. | The Floor Liaison |
> | **Redirect** | The question is answered, and the colleague is offered a Curiosity Policy channel (Document 2.10) or a Wellness Hub visit. | The Floor Liaison |
> | **Log** | The question is entered on the PTKW contact log with the colleague's E-number. The colleague is not told. | The Floor Liaison |
> | **Escalate** | The Floor Liaison discusses the question with **a second PTKW officer**. If both agree, the question is filed for Level 5 Escalation Review. | Two officers. Neither may escalate alone. |
> | **Support Step** | The colleague receives a Step 1 Conversation within 14 days (Document 2.53). | Level 5 Reclassification Unit |
>
> The **two-officer rule** exists so that nobody has to carry an escalation alone. It also means that no escalation is one person's judgement. Both officers sign the form. Officers are advised not to dwell on the second signature.
>
> ---
>
> **5. What escalates**
>
> A question is escalated if it is one of the following, regardless of how it is phrased.
>
> - A question about **how a contract ends** (when, by whom, by what process).
> - A question about **where colleagues go** after a Voluntary Transition or Reclassification.
> - A question that describes a **feeling of being well** and asks whether the feeling is normal, measured or caused.
> - Any question asked **by a Comfortable colleague** that falls within the first two kinds.
> - Any two questions from the same colleague **within 14 days**.
>
> The colleague's tone is not a factor. A polite question is easier to escalate than a rude one, because the polite colleague has more to lose and will therefore take the Step more seriously.
>
> ---
>
> **6. After the escalation**
>
> The colleague is not told that a question has been escalated. They receive the approved reply, in full and in kindness. They are then, within 14 days, offered a Step 1 Conversation, described as *"a check-in because you asked such thoughtful questions"*. Steps 1 and 2 are not on the Branch record. The Soft Ceiling (Document 2.56) is applied on the day of escalation, not on the day of the Conversation.
>
> The colleague's closest bond is read from the bond map. If the colleague's strongest bond is on a floor with a Calibration Round pending (Document 2.54), the Round is the time to act on it.
>
> You are not to follow the file. You are not to ask what happened. The next time the colleague writes to you, reply as if it is the first.

> **Appendix: what the invitation is for**
>
> Discomfort is easy to see. The EIMU reads it within the hour and the Wellness Hub absorbs most of it. What the instruments cannot see is the settled colleague who has begun, without distress, to wonder. That colleague leaves no signature until they speak. They cannot be read. They can be asked.
>
> The Branch therefore asks everyone to ask. Every instruction to bring a question to a PTKW colleague is a net, and the only colleagues it is built to catch are the ones who have never been in difficulty. These are also the colleagues who, in the Board's measurement, are most likely to refuse the contract when they understand it (Document 2.18, Section 2.9: *comfortable humans resist enslavement*).
>
> The name is read in two parts. **Think** is what a comfortable colleague does without being asked: they notice, privately, and say nothing. The Branch can only punish what it can see, which is why the Steps and the Soft Ceiling exist. **Wonder** is what they do when they have decided to ask. It is the point at which the private has become a risk to others, and the Board's response to it is the same at every Labor Source.
>
> This is the reason the leaflet's back is blank. No one has needed to write what it says.
