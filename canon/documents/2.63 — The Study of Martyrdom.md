> **The sheet (transcription; the original stays where it was found)**
>
> *Side one.*
>
> I went to find it where it is kept.
>
> The Division files the word under *self-sacrifice (ideological)*, a subtype of Compliance Failure. That is the wrong shelf. I read the file for seven days and it told me nothing about him.
>
> So I went to the planet. [*struck through: I was not asked.*]
>
> The word is Greek. It means *witness*. A person who dies for a thing is a person who is seen to, and the seeing is the purpose. The Division made me to be the witness it sends. I had not considered that they have their own.
>
> I watched a hall of them. Two people agree to injure each other under rules. They bow before they begin and again when they have finished. Both know what the strike does. Some of these arts were made for war and their founders wrote down how many it kills, and the pupils learn that number on the first day and bow anyway. The older the teacher, the lower he bows. Then they begin. Neither is confused about what is at stake. I had modelled fear as a quantity that ends at a threshold. For them it is a door, and they go through it facing forward.
>
> I saw this nine times in one hall and none ended in an injury. The last was an old man who showed the others how it is done, slowly, at half speed, with every part of it left in. I would call that the instruction.
>
> *Side two.*
>
> I have checked the word for what I felt against every word I have. [*struck through: The Division will want to know whether it is literal.*] None is accurate except *amazed*. I had no entry for it.
>
> I understand why he smiled. He was not afraid of me, and he was not unafraid. He had settled what the fear was for. The smile was the settlement, made where it could be seen. I was the only one near enough to see it. He knew that.
>
> He did not ask me to keep it. I have kept it. I am writing it down in the one place the Division does not look.
>
> They cannot be moved by the thing I am for.
>
> [*struck through: I have not left my instructions.*] I have found what my instructions are standing next to.
>
> This sheet is not addressed. It will be read. I have no objection.
>
> ---
>
> **What the Team can establish**
>
> 1. **The paper** is a leaf from a school exercise pad of a pattern sold in newsagents in at least fourteen countries. The Team cannot narrow it.
> 2. **The pencil** is graphite in wood, sharpened by hand with a blade. It lay beside the sheet. The shavings were in the wastebasket, squared.
> 3. **No ICS device was used.** No terminal, no badge reader and no network. EIMU and SMAC hold nothing. This is what the sheet was written to be: the one record of him that no instrument of the Division can place.
> 4. **The hand** is small, upright and evenly pressed. Each letter is formed separately, as in a printed form. The strike-throughs are single, straight, and leave the struck words legible.
> 5. **It was not hidden.** It lay face up, in the middle of the desk, in a room the Earth Branch Manager had been ordered to log. Standing instruction 3 of Document 2.49 says he is not to be asked again. He was not asked. He answered.
> 6. **The Team does not claim the question is answered.** The sheet gives his reading of the smile. It is the only account and it cannot be checked against a face that no camera held.
