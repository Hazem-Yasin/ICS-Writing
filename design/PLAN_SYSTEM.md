# ICS: Interplanetary Corporate Solutions
## The Plan System — Support Steps, Cover Stories and the Layered Lie

*[PROPOSAL] The logic behind Documents 2.52–2.61 and the messages A-30 to A-37, B-31 to B-36, C-27 to C-30 and design-team 10 to 12. Every real-world HR instrument the author asked for is built here as an ICS instrument with one rule behind all of them. Nothing here overrides canon; where it touches canon, the touch is named.*

---

## 1. The one rule

**The outcome is decided before the process starts. The process exists to make the outcome look like the end of a story.**

An asset chosen for Journey Completion (the Transition Event) is never "managed out". They are *staged*. A plan, a probation, a review or a survey is the stage. Every document on the stage is true in its own words and false in its purpose.

Corollary: **each layer of access sees a reason, and each reason is the cover story for the layer above it.** No reader ever holds a lie; each holds a true statement about a smaller room.

| Layer | Who reads it | What they are shown | What it really is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Staff** | The asset, colleagues, line managers | *Support.* A warm plan, a growth goal, a "no wrong outcome" | The cover story |
| **PTKW** | Level 5 and above | *Deviation.* A BDRI score, a Protocol 7 flag, an outcome code they are told not to alter | The cover story PTKW is allowed to believe |
| **Branch Director** | The Director, the Earth Branch Manager | *Directed timing.* "Disposition basis: HQ." The Branch is told when, never why | A routing slip |
| **Research Team** | Interplanetary Research Division | *Yield.* Bond maps, readiness curves, severance events | The method |
| **Board** | The Board | *Decision.* Set at intake or at a bond-severance event. No reason is recorded because none is needed | The truth |

The player should be able to hold one case in four hands: the form on a desk (Staff), the reverse page on a Level 5 terminal (PTKW), a routing slip on the Director's desk, and a Board stamp that only a Board-level terminal prints. Each one is accurate. None says what the others say.

## 3. The rules that make the lie hold

1. **Disposition before process.** Every Step 5 date is set from the readiness model, not from the asset's performance. The plan's end date is derived from it.
2. **Evidence Alignment.** The file must always support the outcome. Observations are added to the file *after* the decision and dated to fit. No one at any layer below the Research Team is told this.
3. **Completion ceiling.** The goal-attainment display on a plan is capped (80%) until Step 4. A plan can be worked perfectly and still not read as done. PTKW is told this protects motivation.
4. **Non-completion is the design.** A plan that is completed ends the grief, the curiosity or the dependency that made the asset valuable. Every Earth firm benchmarked sees most plans end without completion; ICS does not need to engineer that. It only needs to not interfere. Design-team memo 10 holds the objection.
5. **Perception Alignment.** When an asset's memory of a meeting differs from the record, the record is not corrected; the asset is. See Doc 2.60 and B-36.
6. **Managers are scored on outcomes they are not told about.** The rubric rewards "supported-out" counts (Doc 2.59) and punishes *advocacy* for an asset on a Step. This is why managers in A-series messages sound sincere.
7. **No one says the word.** No Staff document says "PIP", "termination", "performance management" or "dismissal". PTKW documents say "reclassification". Only Board documents say what the Step 5 is for.

## 5. The gaslighting layer: Perception Alignment

Real-world note: gaslighting needs a power gap and a victim who trusts the source. ICS has both. The mechanism is administrative, never theatrical:

- Meeting dates in the record move by a day. The asset's memory of the day is "wrong".
- A Sunday check-in answer (Doc 2.06) is amended after submission; the asset is shown the amended version.
- A sentence the asset said is quoted back, slightly changed.
- A message the asset sent is delivered with one word altered (CCN edit, B-36).
- A colleague who remembers the original is given a warm Step 1 Conversation of their own.

Rule of use: **the player never sees the alteration happen.** They see two documents that disagree by one detail (A-30, A-37) and then find the order that made it so (B-36).
